Memark Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Memark is an expense journal: every expense you record becomes a Mark on your own timeline. This policy explains what Memark does with your information. In short: your Marks stay on your devices and in your own iCloud, and Memark has no servers of its own.

What Memark keeps, and where

  • Your Marks. The amount, merchant, date, category, notes, tags and any photo, receipt scan or voice recording you attach. They're stored on your iPhone. If iCloud is on, they're also kept in your private iCloud database so your other devices have them. Only your Apple Account can read that database. The developer can't.
  • Your settings. Appearance, Quick Mark preferences, Repeats, Trips and your home currency are stored the same way.
  • Things shared with the widget and Share extension. Today's total and your Quick Marks are kept in a shared container on your iPhone so the widget can show them. Photos you share to Memark from another app are kept there until Memark next opens and reads them, then deleted.

What Memark reads, and how

  • Camera. Used only while you're taking a photo for a Mark.
  • Photos. Only the photos you pick or share to Memark are read. Memark doesn't browse your library. It reads when a photo was taken, but not where: location data in photos is never read.
  • Microphone and speech recognition. Used only while you hold the shutter to speak an expense. Speech is transcribed on your iPhone (on-device recognition), never by a server.
  • Receipts and text. Read by iOS (Vision) on your iPhone.
  • Apple Intelligence. Captions, Search and Insights use Apple's on-device model only, never Private Cloud Compute. Nothing you record is sent to a model in the cloud.

What Memark doesn't do

  • No account or sign-in, and no servers of Memark's own.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs, and no advertising identifier.
  • No location tracking and no reverse geocoding.
  • No selling or sharing of your information with anyone.
  • No exchange-rate or other network lookups: amounts in different currencies are never converted.

The only network traffic is Apple's: iCloud syncing your own data, and the App Store handling purchases.

Purchases

Memark Pro and other purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. Memark learns only whether a purchase is active. It never learns your name, email address or payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

Deleting your information

  • Peel a Mark off to delete it, along with its photo and recording.
  • Deleting Memark removes everything stored on your iPhone.
  • To remove Memark's data from iCloud, go to Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › Memark and delete it.

Children

Memark doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children: nothing you record leaves your devices except through your own iCloud.
